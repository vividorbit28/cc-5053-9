Record & reverse-fingering replay (R4)

Records breath pressure, fingering and the model's measured frequency, then replays. Reversing keeps the pressure/timing order and plays the fingering sequence backwards.

History vs from-scratch restart (R3, R6, R7)

Two engines get the identical pressure-only dip. One keeps the wave in the bore; the other is cleared at the dip and restarts from the seed. A dip shorter than the ~100 ms bore decay is the shortest fragment that separates them.

Brief close-restore acceptance (R5)

A note with open holes is briefly closed then restored, with no engine reset. The existing vibration must survive and the note must return.

What this model is and is not

Model

  • Pressure-controlled reed gap (2nd-order mechanics) and a bidirectional valve with a smoothed orifice law, solved implicitly against the tube at the same sample.
  • One travelling-wave delay line with fractional read, a lossy bell reflection, and open-hole effective-length changes. The bore filter shapes the loop; it is not a post-filter on a pre-made tone.
  • 64-bit internal state in the worklet, 2× oversampling, peak and DC protection in the shared output stage.

Limits

  • Uniform bore, lumped reed, no real tonehole lattice: a sound-design model, not an instrument clone.
  • Open holes are modelled by shortening the speaking length; cross-fingering and register holes are not simulated.
  • Pitch depends on blowing pressure (−23 cents at 1.2 kPa, +4 cents at 4.2 kPa, +22 cents at 4.8 kPa). That is a model behaviour, reported and measured, not corrected away.
